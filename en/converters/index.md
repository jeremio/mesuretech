# Converters

Pick a quantity. Conversion happens as you type. The ⇄ button swaps the units, and the page link keeps the value and units you chose, so you can share it.

<CategoryGrid />

Conversion factors come from the official definitions (for example, 1 inch = 0.0254 m exactly). Details for every unit are in the [units guide](/en/guide/units-of-measurement).
